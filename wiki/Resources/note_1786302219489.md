# Global Warming Overview

Global warming is the long-term increase in Earth’s average surface temperature, primarily driven by human activities such as burning fossil fuels (coal, oil, natural gas), deforestation, and industrial processes.  These activities release greenhouse gases like carbon dioxide Password=($CO_2$), methane ($CH_4$), and nitrous oxide ($N_2O$) into the atmosphere, enhancing the greenhouse effect by trapping heat that would otherwise escape into space. 

While climate change refers to the broader suite of changes in weather patterns and ecosystems resulting from this warming, global warming specifically denotes the rise in temperature.  The planet’s average surface temperature has risen approximately 1.1°C to 1.44°C above pre-industrial levels (1850–1900), with the rate of warming accelerating significantly since the mid-20th century.
